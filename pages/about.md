## About the Collection

This archive is designed to provide insight into the daily and religious life in Ancient Etruria. The majority of artifacts are photographs of primary resources or reproductions. Additionally, there are two scholarly archeological accounts providing further context and a springboard for research. For those interested in a general introduction to depictions of Etruscan daily life and culture, simply browsing the collection may prove fruitful. Students of ancient civilizations may find a benefit in conducting comparative analysis  with other items housed in similarly-positioned digital collections concerning other ancient civilizations.
